💡 Student Money Hacks
Tried-and-tested tips from student travellers
  • 🏦 Use Wise or Revolut — the best exchange rates abroad, zero hidden fees, and instant conversion between currencies.
  • 🎓 Always carry your student ID — most global museums, monuments, and even transport give 20–50% discounts.
  • 🕐 Travel off-peak — flights booked 6–8 weeks ahead on Tue/Wed are cheapest. Avoid school holiday weeks.
  • 🍜 Eat 2 streets from tourist sites — same food, 30–50% cheaper. Look for the queues of locals, not tourists.
  • 🏨 Hostels aren't just dorms — they have social kitchens, free tours, and connect you with fellow travellers. Often 70% cheaper than hotels.
  • 📲 Download maps offline — Maps.me and Google Maps offline work without data. Essential for budget SIM cards abroad.
